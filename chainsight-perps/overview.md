# Overview

* **The Gap:** Crypto traders have been limited to directional bets on token prices, missing 95% of what makes traditional markets interesting
* **The Problem:** Even sophisticated DeFi lacks basic TradFi instruments like volatility products, major indices, and cross-asset hedges
* **Our Solution:** Purpose-built oracles and data pipelines that can turn any market metric into a perpetual contract

## **What Makes Us Different**

* **Data-First Approach:** We engineer custom oracle solutions and data pipelines tailored to each asset class.
* **Institutional Grade:** Our variance calculations, index methodologies, and price feeds meet professional trading standards.
* **Beyond Crypto:** Access S\&P 500, EUR/USD, or Bitcoin volatility - all from your Hyperliquid wallet.
