# HIP-3 Context & Integration

**Building on Hyperliquid's Permissionless Future**

HIP-3 represents the democratization of market creation. By staking 1M HYPE, builders can deploy perpetual markets using Hyperliquid's world-class infrastructure - matching engine, liquidity, and one-block finality.

**The ChainSight Approach**

Unlike builders racing to list meme tokens or replicate existing crypto markets, ChainSight focuses on bringing institutional-grade financial instruments on-chain:

* **Capital Efficiency:** Each market we deploy targets proven demand from our TradFi/institutional partners (e.g., Variance and volatility instruments, forex, gold, stock indices)
* **Risk Awareness:** Launching with hedging instruments (variance perps) to address risk strategy needs of our partners.&#x20;
* **Oracle Excellence:** Every market is powered by robust, manipulation-resistant data feeds
