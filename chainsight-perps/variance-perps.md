# Variance Perps

## **Trade Volatility, Not Direction**

Variance perpetuals are ChainSight's flagship innovation - the first clean volatility exposure in DeFi through perpetual contracts.

## **What Are Variance Perps?**

* **VAR-BTC/VAR-ETH:** Track 30-day realized volatility of Bitcoin and Ethereum
* **Pure Volatility Play:** Profit from market turbulence regardless of price direction
* **No Greeks, No Expiries:** Unlike options, variance perps are simple - volatility goes up, longs profit

## **Why Traders Need This**

* **Hidden Opportunities:** The variance risk premium in crypto represents \~$45B in "free money" previously inaccessible to most traders
* **Professional Hedging:** Market makers can hedge volatility exposure without juggling options strikes and expiries
* **Regime Trading:** Position for calm-to-chaos transitions without predicting price direction

## **Use Cases**

* **Pre-Event Positioning:** Long volatility before major announcements
* **Portfolio Protection:** Hedge against volatility spikes that hurt options books
* **Pure Speculation:** Trade your view on whether markets become more or less volatile
